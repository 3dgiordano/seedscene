# Building docs/PLAN-v0.2.md, a small piece at a time

The owner approved the whole plan for v0.2 on 2026-10-28 and asked to build it in small pieces.

Done, each checked in rendered frames, measured for cost, tested, in the gallery and the docs:

- the fire (DOS)
- the Kefrens bars (Amiga)
- the bouncing checkered ball (Amiga)
- the interference rings (Amiga, DOS)
- the LCD juggler (arcade) - rebuilt against photos of the real 1980 device after the owner found the first version unrecognisable; moved from the handheld era so it keeps its silver and red
- the scanner car (arcade)
- the footsteps (32-bit console) - redrawn against frames of the scene after the owner asked never to work from memory
- the twister (Amiga) - built from a reference still of an Amiga twister demo
- the pyramid hopper (arcade) - built from the game's screenshot
- the 3D pipes (DOS) - built from three captures of the screensaver
- the unlimited bobs (Amiga) - built from three videos of the effect, then rebuilt on the original's algorithm (cycled screens, the chain sliding behind its head) after the owner's look; music-driven, a twin chain; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29
- the dual chessboard and the chessboard floor (Amiga) - the chessboard zoomer, split in two at the owner's word: Nuance's dual boards, now turning on their axes with Sanity's fluid zoom; and Quadriga's floor with its bouncing cube and a wave; the owner approved dualchess on 2026-10-29 after it was calmed (no small squares rushing), chessfloor approved too after the cube was smoothed and the scene given depth
- the rubber vector (Amiga) - built from a video of the effect and the vector slime article (each line showing an older copy of the cube); given the music response all our scenes have; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29
- the bump-mapped logo (DOS) - built from the algorithm in Sol's Graphics Tutorial (no faithful still of the classic look found in videos); a plate in perspective and flat blue shadows after the owner's look; approved 2026-10-29
- the cracktro (Amiga) - built from frames of Skid Row, Paradox and Fairlight crack intros; after the owner's look: a 3D starfield flown through on the music, project texts and thanks to 3dgiordano and the community, the intro only at the start, no scroller of its own; warps forward or sideways at each phrase end, glowing stars; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29
- the card cascade (Y2K, standing in for the media-player years) - built from a still and two animated captures of the original's win screen; a card a bar and another mid-bar when the music is busy (the owner chose a card a bar over the plan's card a beat on 2026-10-30); first landing on the beat, the "and" or the sixteenth; livelier bounces when busy; a new deal every phrase; the Y2K glow turned down to a quarter for this scene so the one-pixel edges stay black; after the owner's look (too static) given the stage and kick punch, trails tinted by each kick and snare, aging, shadows, landing sparks, woven felt and a colour run in each phrase's last bar; the owner approved it on 2026-10-30
- the ANSI board (DOS) - built from ANSI works of a June 1995 pack in the 16colo.rs archive; a line a beat, a part of the file a phrase, the logo complete on the drop; after the owner's look the camera eases in and out (no swing, a fifth closer at most), the scroll is smooth; the full punch with a push-in on each kick; the seed word up to twelve letters; the owner approved it on 2026-10-30
- the bouncing lines (DOS) - the second screensaver, built from still and animated captures; faster as the music builds, the kick stretching the echoes and punching, the snare moving the colours on, more echoes when busy; the owner approved it on 2026-10-30
- the 3D maze (DOS) - the last screensaver, built from captures of the 95 maze; a move every two beats, corners taken walking, a footfall and the punch on the kick, the snare lighting the ceiling, a stone before each drop turning the world over; the owner approved it on 2026-10-30
- the moon bike (modern) - built from stills of the film's moon shot; after the owner's look: the cape as cloth, a camera panning and zooming over layers in parallax, the scene opening with the bicycle riding in (a scene now knows when it came on), and two bars out of the frame between crossings; the owner approved it on 2026-10-30
- the time car (arcade) - rebuilt mid-way at the owner's word from stills of the film's first run: the parking lot, a 3D car lapping it and charging between the onlookers, 88 on the music's big moments; after the owner's look: fire and plate only after a jump, a true reverse and a plate insert, the plate spinning like a coin, the onlookers moving; the owner approved it on 2026-10-31

Approved on 2026-10-31:
- skatepunk, the music style (round 11) - ska verses, punk choruses (a punk drumming lesson's beats), a shouted synth chorus within an octave; on the anime opening's song form
- animeop, the music style (round 11) - the anison's form from a corpus study of anime openings; Royal Road chorus, the chorus maybe a minor third up, the last chorus pumped up; J-rock with brass on the AWE32

Also approved on 2026-10-31 (round 12 done):
- the sounds between the tracks - each era's chime opening it in THE JOURNEY, a cartridge clicking in before handheld tracks, a modem handshake into Y2K (from an annotated spectrogram); a layer over the mix
- screensaver, the third way to watch - a minute without input, AUTOPLAY with nothing but the picture; any key back; full screen only if the page already was (browsers allow it only after a gesture)
- greetings, the second way to watch - G and names live, --greets for a video; the scroller, the About greetings and the cracktro's pages; kept after the address's #, never sent
- born in, the first way to watch - B and the year live, --born on the command line; AUTOPLAY opens in the era of the twelfth year and comes back every other block
- triphop, the music style (DOS, Y2K) - from a trip-hop drum cheat sheet and the genre's description: the shuffled, pitched-down break, a deep sliding bass, dark strings, Rhodes, theremin, scratches, crackle
- keygen, the music style (DOS, Y2K) - from the pattern data of 40 real keygen modules: 125 bpm most often, the arpeggio effect on the tick, a squelchy sliding lead with an echo channel, a bouncing bass; the 24 earlier styles byte-identical
- the anime opening (32-bit console) - from frames of two openings of the era; a cut on every bar, poses on the beat, the kick punching, the title slamming in on the drop; reworked at the owner's look (2026-10-31): the flight's clouds right to left, the power-up a two-bar charge, burst and energized aura from animated references; then lightning and a muscular figure that bulks up

Also rebuilt from references at the owner's request: the starfighter (console) - its white rays were background stars passing too close to the camera; six screenshots of the original; the owner confirmed it on 2026-10-29.

Reworked at the owner's request on 2026-10-29, each confirmed by the owner live:

- every game scene floats on the stage (the LCD juggler as the whole device), with the kick punch
