# Building docs/PLAN-v0.2.md, a small piece at a time

The owner approved the whole plan for v0.2 on 2026-10-28 and asked to build it in small pieces.

Done, each checked in rendered frames, measured for cost, tested, in the gallery and the docs:

- the fire (DOS)
- the Kefrens bars (Amiga)
- the bouncing checkered ball (Amiga)
- the interference rings (Amiga, DOS)
- the LCD juggler (arcade) - rebuilt against photos of the real 1980 device after the owner found the first version unrecognisable; moved from the handheld era so it keeps its silver and red
- the scanner car (arcade)
- the footsteps (32-bit console) - redrawn against frames of the scene after the owner asked never to work from memory
- the twister (Amiga) - built from a reference still of an Amiga twister demo
- the pyramid hopper (arcade) - built from the game's screenshot
- the 3D pipes (DOS) - built from three captures of the screensaver
- the unlimited bobs (Amiga) - built from three videos of the effect, then rebuilt on the original's algorithm (cycled screens, the chain sliding behind its head) after the owner's look; music-driven, a twin chain; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29
- the dual chessboard and the chessboard floor (Amiga) - the chessboard zoomer, split in two at the owner's word: Nuance's dual boards, now turning on their axes with Sanity's fluid zoom; and Quadriga's floor with its bouncing cube and a wave; the owner approved dualchess on 2026-10-29 after it was calmed (no small squares rushing), chessfloor approved too after the cube was smoothed and the scene given depth
- the rubber vector (Amiga) - built from a video of the effect and the vector slime article (each line showing an older copy of the cube); given the music response all our scenes have; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29
- the bump-mapped logo (DOS) - built from the algorithm in Sol's Graphics Tutorial (no faithful still of the classic look found in videos); a plate in perspective and flat blue shadows after the owner's look; approved 2026-10-29
- the cracktro (Amiga) - built from frames of Skid Row, Paradox and Fairlight crack intros; after the owner's look: a 3D starfield flown through on the music, project texts and thanks to 3dgiordano and the community, the intro only at the start, no scroller of its own; warps forward or sideways at each phrase end, glowing stars; the owner approved it on 2026-10-29

Also rebuilt from references at the owner's request: the starfighter (console) - its white rays were background stars passing too close to the camera; six screenshots of the original; the owner confirmed it on 2026-10-29.

Reworked at the owner's request on 2026-10-29, each confirmed by the owner live:
- the 3D pipes - a simulation on a deep grid, filled back to front, elbows, sixteen bars to saturate
- every game scene floats on the stage (the LCD juggler as the whole device), with the kick punch
- the pyramid - played as a simulation of the arcade game (red balls, the egg and the snake, discs, the swearing on a hit), on the beat
- the starfighter - drones from the horizon or the distance, never out of the ground; no roll deflecting shots; a hit shakes the ship and flashes once

Still to build, by round of the plan (section 8): round 5 - the YM2149 chip, seven styles, glenz, landscape, the musicdisk player and mode; round 11 - three styles and ten scenes (vector tube, wireframe tank, road crossing, behind-view shooter, cartoon castle, artillery, skate park, football match, kart race, DJ decks); round 12 - two styles, the other screensavers, card cascade, ANSI board, time car, moon bike, anime opening, three modes, the sounds between tracks; rounds 1-4, 6-10, 13, 14 not started.

The next piece is the owner's pick, or Claude's if they leave it open.
